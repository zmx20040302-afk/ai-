## A. 基线更正（**现在可审**；这是 v11 里的事实错误）

### A.1 错在哪

v11 补充材料 §S9 的表格说明写着：

> "⚠️ **Only the 20 MHz row shares the fit's caliber and carrier.** The other four residuals come from
> §S9's *17-face* caliber on a ***different* carrier** whose **`ftet1` size is not recorded anywhere**,
> so those rows are a cross-caliber, cross-carrier migration combined with the `h²` extrapolation —
> **not** measurements on this grid."

**两处错**（由 2026-10-13 的零求解侦察实测发现）：

1. 那不是**另一个载体**，而是**同一个载体**（`s34_h1um_out_Model.mph`）的**另一张网格**上的旧解；
2. 该载体的 `ftet1` **现在已登记 = `lamda/5`**（不再是"未记录"）。

**证据**（同一模型文件内两个解的规模差）：

| 解 | DOF | 频点 | 求解日期 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `sol1` | **359,711** | 5/10/15/20/25/30/35 MHz | 2026-09-09 |
| `sol4` | **900,431** | 20 MHz | 2026-10-06 |

⇒ §S9 那 4 行取自 `sol1`（模型自带较粗网格）；载体**当前**网格上的解是 `sol4`。

### A.2 建议替换文（英文，可直接用）

> ⚠️ **Only the 20 MHz row shares the fit's caliber and mesh.** The other four residuals are the
> *17-face* caliber on the **same carrier but a different mesh**: they come from an older solution of
> that model file whose degrees of freedom are **359,711**, whereas the carrier's current mesh solves
> to **900,431**. Re-measuring the whole band on the current mesh (same carrier, same `ftet1` =
> λ/5, 17-face caliber, orientation re-derived per face) gives **0.0567 % (5 MHz), 0.4075 % (10),
> 0.9750 % (15), 1.7286 % (20), 2.5965 % (25), 3.6645 % (30), 4.9503 % (35)** — so those four rows
> should be replaced by these measurements. The 20 MHz entry is verified from both directions: at the
> 16-face caliber this run gives 1.770575 % against the independently recorded 1.770575 % (relative
> difference 3.9e-10), and at the 17-face caliber it gives 1.7286 %, matching the carrier's current-mesh
> solution.

> **A data-hygiene point worth stating once**: a single model file can contain solutions computed on
> **different meshes**, so solutions must be compared by degrees of freedom, not by file name.

### A.3 正文是否需要同步改？

**不需要。** 正文 v11 的对应句说的是"the rest of the 5–35 MHz band has not been brought to that
resolution"，这与基线更正**不冲突**；正文没有引用那 4 行的具体数值。⇒ **v12 的正文改动只在 B 部分。**
